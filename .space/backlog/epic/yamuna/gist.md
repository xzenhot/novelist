# Yamuna - Gist

## Gist

The Delhi NCR is built by Yamuna from prehistorycal days. Yamuna has witnessed the rise of dynasty, pandav nagar, indrapratha, qutub minar, lalqila, now yamuna has become tired, her walk has become slow and dry, asking a question about rubbish from mankind on her chest

## Expansion

Yamuna is the speaking presence of this poetry book: river, mother, witness, archive, road, wound, and judge. Across prehistory, Indraprastha, Sultanate Delhi, Mughal Shahjahanabad, colonial planning, and the present-day NCR, she watches human power build its houses, forts, prayers, drains, markets, and monuments beside her. Her central conflict is not against one villain but against a civilization that praises her as sacred while placing sewage, plastic, concrete, industrial discharge, and forgetfulness upon her chest. The transformation is a movement from mute endurance to a final moral question: whether mankind can still hear a river whose walk has become slow, dry, and tired.
