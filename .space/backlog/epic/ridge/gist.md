# Gist — Ridge

## One-Line Gist

The Aravalli Ridge is a rare tropical forest spine cutting through the concrete heart of Delhi NCR, where the frantic rat race of human ambition collides with — and quietly surrenders to — an ancient ecosystem where animals, birds, and wild folk endure together.

## Expansion

At the edge of one of the world's most populated urban corridors, a broken chain of forested ridges — Mangar Bani, Jasola Bhatti, Buddh Jayanti Park, the JNU campus greens, and the Okhla Bird Sanctuary — holds its ground against encroachment, noise, and neglect. These are not parks in the manicured sense; they are living remnants of a Precambrian landscape older than the Himalayas, still sheltering leopards, nilgai, migratory birds, and the memory of water. The book is a meditation on sustainability as resistance: what it means for a forest to survive a megacity, and what the megacity loses — and occasionally remembers — when it pauses to listen to the ridge.
