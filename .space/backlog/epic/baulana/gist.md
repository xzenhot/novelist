# Gist — baulana

**Gist:** Baul is the highest Sufi of the world — the living form of God's presence, dwelling in the self, revealed through the doctrine of the human body.

**Expansion:** The Baul is the wandering minstrel of Bengal who carries no scripture but the body itself, teaching that the Beloved is not in the mosque or the temple but seated within the cage of flesh. The conflict is the soul's search for the *Moner Manush* — the Man of the Heart — hidden behind the veil of the senses, a search that turns the body from a prison into a temple. The transformation is the awakening of the seeker, who learns that the physical form is not the enemy of the divine but its very dwelling, and that to know God is to know the self.
