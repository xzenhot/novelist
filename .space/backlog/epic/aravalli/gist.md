# Aravalli

## Gist

The mountain ranges evidences for human civilization in Delhi from Prehistoric days, to Vedic age, the modern Delhi

## Expansion

The Aravalli range — the oldest fold mountains on Earth, older than the Himalayas — is the silent spine of Delhi's human story. The book follows a geologist and a historian who trace how the same ridge of quartzite and granite has witnessed every layer of the city's civilization: the stone tools of prehistoric hunters, the Vedic fire-altars and forest hermitages, the medieval forts and water-harvesting stepwells, and the concrete sprawl of modern Delhi. The conflict is not a battle of armies but a struggle between memory and erasure, as the ancient ridge is quarried, flattened, and buried beneath the very city it made possible. The transformation is the reader's — and the protagonists' — realization that a mountain is not a backdrop but a living archive, and that to lose the ridge is to lose the evidence of who Delhi has been.
