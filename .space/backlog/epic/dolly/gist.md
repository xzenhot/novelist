# Dolly - Gist

## Gist

A beautiful woman from New Delhi, a lovable for a poet as she looked like Radha in Yamuna , with pitcher in the lap

## Expansion

Dolly is the luminous figure at the center of this poetry sequence: a woman from New Delhi whose presence awakens a poet's devotional imagination. The conflict is not a plot of possession, but a spiritual and aesthetic tension between earthly beauty and sacred vision, between the city's restless modern life and the ancient image of Radha by the Yamuna with a pitcher in her lap. Across the poems, the poet learns to love without reducing Dolly to metaphor, transforming desire into reverence, memory, and disciplined song.
