# Gist — Ashok

**Gist:** A tormented prince-turned-emperor seeks redemption after a brutal conquest, turning from the sword to the dharma and reshaping an empire through compassion.

**Expansion:** Ashok, third son of the Mauryan emperor Bindusara, is passed over for succession and hardened by frontier wars. After seizing the throne through a bloody coup, he conquers Kalinga in a pyrrhic victory that shatters him. Haunted by the dead, he slowly converts to the Buddha's teachings and attempts to rule by dharma — banning slaughter, planting herbs, digging wells, sending missionaries, and inscribing his confessions on stone. The novel follows his inner war between violent habit and ethical empire, between memory and forgetting, between the sword and the dharma.
