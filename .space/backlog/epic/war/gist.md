# Gist — War

**Gist:** A geopolitical thriller about a modern war erupting over control of the Strait of Hormuz, tracing the choices of sailors, pilots, civilians, and leaders as the global economy teeters on the edge of collapse.

**Expansion:** A mysterious explosion sinks a Panama-flagged oil tanker in the Strait of Hormuz, blocking the world's most important energy chokepoint. Within hours, oil prices spike, markets panic, and old alliances strain. As naval forces converge, a cyber weapon of unknown origin turns the conflict into a war fought as much in silicon as in steel. The novel follows an American destroyer executive officer, an Iranian naval captain, a downed fighter pilot, a SEAL rescue team, and civilians on both shores as each tries to survive choices made by leaders they will never meet.
