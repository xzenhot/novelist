## Premise

A mysterious explosion sinks a Panama-flagged oil tanker in the Strait of Hormuz, blocking the world's most important energy chokepoint. Within hours, oil prices spike, markets panic, and old alliances strain. As naval forces converge, a cyber weapon of unknown origin turns the conflict into a war fought as much in silicon as in steel. The novel follows an American destroyer executive officer, an Iranian naval captain, a downed fighter pilot, a SEAL rescue team, and civilians on both shores as each tries to survive choices made by leaders they will never meet.

## Setting

Primary locations: the Strait of Hormuz, Bandar Abbas, the USS Truxtun, the USS Nimitz carrier strike group, Washington D.C., London, Dubai, Tehran, and southern Iran. The action unfolds over several weeks as a contained incident escalates into a full naval and cyber war.

## Main Characters

- **Commander Sarah Lin** — Executive Officer of the USS Truxtun, calm under pressure, responsible for holding the line when the strait becomes a battlefield.
- **Captain Reza Sharifi** — Aging, pragmatic IRGCN officer watching the chaos from Qeshm Island; he understands the cost of escalation better than his superiors.
- **Dr. Maya Thorne** — London energy economist whose models predict catastrophic global recession if the strait stays closed.
- **Marcus Vance** — U.S. National Security Advisor, balancing military deterrence with the last off-ramps of diplomacy.
- **Lieutenant Commander "Viper" Morales** — F/A-18E Super Hornet pilot flying suppression-of-enemy-air-defenses missions over the Iranian coast.
- **Master Chief Miller** — SEAL team leader inserted behind enemy lines to rescue Morales after his aircraft is shot down.
- **Amir** — Disillusioned Iranian cyber-warfare technician who realizes the algorithm he helped write is spinning out of control.
- **Zahra** — Nurse in Bandar Abbas working double shifts as civilian infrastructure collapses around her.
- **Captain Thomas Vance** — Civilian master of the trapped supertanker *Orion Pride*, managing panicking crew and dwindling supplies.

## Thematic Threads

- The illusion of control in complex geopolitical systems.
- The distance between decision-makers and the people who suffer the consequences.
- How cyber and economic warfare blur the line between soldier and civilian.
- Survival, professionalism, and small acts of mercy amid systemic violence.
- The price of reopening a broken world: no winners, only survivors.

## Chapter Outline

1. **The Spark in Dhow Lane** — A tanker explodes in the Strait of Hormuz; Commander Lin and Captain Sharifi converge on the disaster as minor skirmishes begin.
2. **Shockwaves in Markets and Capitals** — Oil prices spike; governments scramble; a cyberattack blacks out port infrastructure across the UAE.
3. **Asymmetric Shadows** — Iran deploys mines, speedboat swarms, and coastal anti-ship missiles; the USS Truxtun defends minesweepers under fire.
4. **The Ghost in the Machine** — Amir discovers his own code may have triggered the tanker incident; cyber warfare cripples power grids and military networks.
5. **The Strait Closes** — Iran declares the strait closed to all traffic; civilian tankers are trapped, and a boarding action puts The Orion Pride in danger.
6. **Escalation Dominance** — Operation "Open Gate" begins; Morales leads SEAD missions until his wingman is shot down.
7. **Behind Enemy Lines** — Master Chief Miller's team races to rescue the downed pilot before Iranian security forces close in.
8. **The Human Cost on the Home Front** — Zahra evacuates a hospital under coastal strikes; global fuel riots and supply shortages spread.
9. **The Decisive Battle of the Chokepoint** — The coalition clears the strait in a devastating engagement; Lin and Sharifi match wits across radar screens.
10. **Dawn Over an Empty Sea** — A fragile ceasefire opens the strait to limping traffic; survivors face a changed world order.
