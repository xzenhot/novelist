# Workshop Metadata — Wife: The Keeper of the Hearth, the Quiet Strength of a Life

## Workshop Team

The workshop is a gathering of storytellers who frame each chapter of the novel. They discuss the story of the wife — her promise, her home, her children, her storms, and her return — and respond to it after it is told.

## Narrated-Story Figures

- **The Wife** — the protagonist, the keeper of the hearth.
- **The Husband** — her partner through the long years.
- **The Children** — the sons and daughters who carry her memory.
- **The Elders** — the community that arranges the marriage and passes down tradition.

## Schedule

| Chapter | Title | Focus |
|---------|-------|-------|
| Introduction | The Promise | The first promise and the beginning of a home. |
| 1 | The Building of the Home | The small rituals that hold a life together. |
| 2 | The Children | A new kind of love, and the memory of the family. |
| 3 | The Storms | Sickness, want, and the quiet strength that endures. |
| 4 | The Return | The love given away returns, multiplied. |
| Conclusion | The Hearth | The closing of the arc — the wife as the quiet strength of a life. |

## Grounding Notes

- The story is grounded in the universal experience of marriage and the home.
- The wife is the memory of the family, the keeper of its story.
- The home is made not of walls but of love, and the wife is its keeper.
