# Research Filter

## Purpose

Ground each chapter in accurate subject matter: era, place, figures, events, and sources.

## Inputs

- `filters/1/<chapter>.md` — the workshop narrative for the chapter.
- `.space/backlog/epic/<bookname>/epic.md` — the epic source of truth.
- External reference material as required by the subject.

## Outputs

- `filters/2/<chapter>.md` — per-chapter research notes.
- Each note documents: subject, era, place, figures, events, grounding notes, and sources.

## Rules

1. Verify all factual claims against reliable sources.
2. Note uncertainties; do not invent historical detail.
3. Keep research focused on what the chapter needs, not the whole book.

## Hand-off

The next filter (`3`) consumes research notes to build character and quality seeds.
