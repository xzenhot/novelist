# Workshop Metadata — War

## Workshop Frame

Every chapter uses the three-section structure:

1. **Workshop** — Grounding, context, and the questions the chapter must answer.
2. **Story** — The narrated scene or sequence, at least 4,500 words for main chapters.
3. **Discussion** — Reflection, thematic closure, and hand-off to the next chapter.

## Recurring Workshop Roles

- **Historian / Geopolitical analyst** — verifies setting, institutions, and chain of command.
- **Character psychologist** — tracks POV figures across chapters.
- **Theme keeper** — ensures each chapter reflects one of the five thematic threads.
- **Style editor** — holds the gibran syntax register.

## Narrated Story Figures

Commander Sarah Lin, Captain Reza Sharifi, Dr. Maya Thorne, Marcus Vance, Lieutenant Commander Viper Morales, Master Chief Miller, Amir, Zahra, Captain Thomas Vance.

## Schedule / Sequence

Filters run in order: workshop → research → seeds → correctness → theme → syntax → override → quality.

## Grounding Notes

- Avoid real-world factional triumphalism; let both sides have professional competence and human cost.
- Technical details (ships, aircraft, cyber systems) should be plausible, not propaganda.
- The economy is a character: oil prices, shipping lanes, and port blackouts drive decisions in capitals.
