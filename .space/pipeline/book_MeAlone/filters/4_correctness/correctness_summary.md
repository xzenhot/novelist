## Corrections Made

1. **Epic — Historical Grounding (Buddha's teaching).** The original text read *"The Buddha taught that the self is not a fixed thing but a flowing."* This is a loose gloss that slightly misstates the doctrine. The Buddha's actual teaching is **anatta** (no-self) — that there is *no* fixed, permanent self, and that all things are in constant flux. Corrected to: *"The Buddha taught that there is no fixed, permanent self — that all things are in constant flux — and that freedom lies in seeing through the illusion of a separate, enduring self."*
