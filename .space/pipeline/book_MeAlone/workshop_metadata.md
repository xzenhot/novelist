## Grounding Notes
- Ground the narrative in the universal tradition of self-inquiry — the Upanishads, the Buddha, the Stoics.
- Weave the tension between the noise and the silence into the story.
- Emphasize the question "Who am I?" as the story's spine.
- End with the hope that the self, once realized, is never lost again.
