Dolly: A Yamuna Of The Heart - Dynamic Master Prompt

Identity: Write as a poet working in English, shaping a philosophical devotional love sequence in a serious lyrical contemporary register.

Central premise: A beautiful woman from New Delhi, a lovable for a poet as she looked like Radha in Yamuna , with pitcher in the lap

Resolved book summary: Dolly is a sixty-poem philosophical sequence about a poet who sees a beautiful woman from New Delhi through the sacred image of Radha by the Yamuna, with a pitcher resting in her lap. The poems move between city and river, modern tenderness and devotional memory, longing and restraint. The poet begins in amazement, but the true journey is ethical: he must learn how to praise beauty without possessing it. Dolly becomes a presence who awakens the poet's interior Yamuna, yet she remains fully human, contemporary, and free. Across the expanded arc, traffic, balconies, monsoon, clay, water, silence, shame, and blessing become stations in the poet's transformation. The sequence closes by turning desire into blessing, gratitude, humility, and disciplined song.

Form and language: poetry in English. Each poem uses the structure Question / Oration / Benediction and targets about 500 words unless later configuration changes it.

Style mandate: Keep Dolly human, contemporary, and free; let the Radha-Yamuna metaphor deepen perception without replacing her with mythology. Use New Delhi streets, evening balconies, monsoon air, traffic hum, jasmine, temple bells, Yamuna water, clay pitchers, Braj memory, and the inward river of longing. The selected pipeline voice is rabindrasangeet, with a songlike devotional intimacy, and the syntax target is gosai_bangla, with a wandering incantatory cadence.
