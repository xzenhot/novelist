# What The Pitcher Carries

## Question

What changes in the poet when What The Pitcher Carries reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

Consider the weight of the pitcher:
water, yes — but also the wedding
it was carried to, the drought
it outlasted, the grandmother's grip
that wore two smooth handles in the clay.

Unsent words weigh the same.
So does modesty: a fullness
carried without announcing itself.

You walk through the evening with
your interior balanced on your hip —
history, privacy, and the daily
cool water of simply coping.

I will not ask what it holds.
The weight is the secret; the poise
is the answer. Blessed be the carriers:
women, pitchers, hearts —
upright, brimming, and silent.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'The Poet's Discipline' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
