# The Poet's Discipline

## Question

What changes in the poet when The Poet's Discipline reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

Before I write, I take the vow of craft:
no word that would embarrass her
if she ever read it aloud.

Cut the adjective that appraises.
Cut the simile that searches her body.
Cut the tender public claim
that would make her flinch at breakfast.

A poem is a room she may enter;
furnish it with respect or burn it down.

So composition becomes a moral practice:
every line weighed like a gift
wrapped for someone you admire —
nothing borrowed, nothing showy,
nothing that assumes.

This is why the song is quiet, Dolly.
It is not timid. It is careful,
the way hands are careful
with another person's earthenware.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'Radha Beyond Ornament' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
