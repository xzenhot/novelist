# The Poet's Jealousy

## Question

What changes in the poet when The Poet's Jealousy reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

Yes — jealousy came to visit,
sat down in my chest like a creditor,
itemized your hours: who spoke with you,
who made you laugh, who walked
the stretch of road I had renounced.

Ugly arithmetic. I let it finish.
Then I saw what it was:
not love for you, but a wound
in the one who looks —
possession's thumbprint on a free face.

Jealousy, sit down. Be examined.
You are not devotion; you are debt.

And humility came after, with water,
and washed the ledger clean:
she was never mine to lose.
Her freedom was the whole gift,
and I was trying to refuse it.

The wound closes where the claim ends.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'The Garland Refused' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
