# The City As Witness

## Question

What changes in the poet when The City As Witness reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

The city watched me change:
the tea-stall regulars noticed the smile
I no longer hid; the beggar at the flyover
nodded as if he had forecast it;
the crowd at ITO kept parting
exactly wide enough for my patience.

Delhi is a blunt, honest witness.
It does not flatter the transforming.
It simply makes room — a seat,
a gap in traffic, a served glass —
the way a courtroom makes room for truth.

And you were there, in the middle
of the public years, crossing
with your bag and your errands,
a private sun in a public sky.

Let the city testify, then:
the man who praised without claiming
became, at last, capable of it.
The witness rests. The record stands.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'The Beloved's Laughter' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
