# A Blessing At The Riverbank

## Question

What changes in the poet when A Blessing At The Riverbank reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

This chapter's specific charge is: The poet blesses rather than claims the beloved. The riverbank becomes a threshold between longing and release.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'The Last Question Of Desire' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
