# Radha Beyond Ornament

## Question

What changes in the poet when Radha Beyond Ornament reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

The calendar-post Radha smiles
in silver foil over the sweet shop,
decorated into a saleswoman of bliss —
and something in me protests.

Radha was not ornament.
She was a woman of terrible attention,
whose love was a discipline of fire,
who taught the god himself
the meaning of the word apart.

Forgive us, lady of Braj:
we reduced your seriousness to merchandise.

So when I say Dolly resembles you,
I mean this: not the foil, not the pose,
but the unblinking inwardness,
the love that needs no audience.

Restore the image to its weight.
Let reverence replace decoration.
Then, and only then, may my song
stand in the long line of your singers.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'Dolly Beyond Comparison' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
