# Sacred Ordinary Morning

## Question

What changes in the poet when Sacred Ordinary Morning reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

Morning sets out its liturgy:
water boiling, one spoon of tea,
the milk-man's bell, the plant
watered before the plant is thanked,
sweeps that begin where the sweep ended.

Repetition — the city's secret meditation.
No incense, no conch, no audience,
and yet holiness hides in the doing,
in motion repeated past boredom
into something the priests can't price.

She passed with her office bag,
same lane, same hour, same quick nod —
an unannounced grace in a repeat episode.

And I thought: this is Vrindavan's lesson
translated into Delhi's grammar —
the eternal returns as routine,
the daily round as pilgrimage.

Blessed be errands. Blessed be the encore
of ordinary mornings. Blessed be the eyes
that find the altar in the alarm clock.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'The Gaze Washed Clean' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
