# The Threshold

## Question

What changes in the poet when The Threshold reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

Every door in this city is a question:
the doctor's door, the lender's door,
the neighbor's door left open on trust,
your door, which I have never touched.

I stand at thresholds and learn arithmetic:
one step forward is approach;
two steps back is honor;
standing still, with a bowed head,
is a whole philosophy of the between.

The balcony is a threshold; the stairwell too;
the moment before speech is a doorway of air.

Teach me, crossings of Delhi,
the ethics of the almost —
when to arrive, when to pause,
when to turn back and be glad.

Some doors bless us most
by remaining closed.
I knock on none of yours, Dolly.
I only keep your sill swept.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'What The Pitcher Carries' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
