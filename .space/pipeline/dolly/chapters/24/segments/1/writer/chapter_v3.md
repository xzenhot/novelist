# The Flute Not Heard

## Question

What changes in the poet when The Flute Not Heard reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

The flute is silent in this song.
And its silence is a thinner, truer music —
the note a note implies,
the door slightly ajar.

Between your leaving and returning
the air holds its shape like water
in a steady hand.

I will not fill the gap with speculation.
Let the unsounded stay unsounded;
suggestion is a courtesy
paid to what is whole in itself.

Listen: between two windchimes,
between one lamp and the next,
the pause conducts itself —
an orchestra of almost,
a raga of restraint.

What is suggested, never seized,
keeps ringing in me
longer than any played note.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'Prayer Without Temple' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
