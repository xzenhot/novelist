# A Dupatta Like Water

## Question

What changes in the poet when A Dupatta Like Water reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

A blue dupatta crosses the crowded lane
and the lane becomes Yamuna for a moment —
not replacing you, Dolly, never that,
only admitting what moves through you.

Metaphor is a dupatta itself:
it covers, it colors, it must not strangle.
I throw the cloth of the river over my seeing
and take it off again at your door,
because the woman inside is more
than any water I can borrow.

Cloth, motion, current, grace:
the world braids its images around you
like a mother braiding a child's hair —
ornament that knows it is not the head.

Let my figures sit lightly on you.
You are the face; the river is only fabric.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'The Threshold' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
