# Dolly Free In Delhi

## Question

What changes in the poet when Dolly Free In Delhi reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

This chapter's specific charge is: Dolly moves through her own life, untouched by the poet's claim. The poem lets her freedom become the final image of beauty.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'Song Without Possession' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
