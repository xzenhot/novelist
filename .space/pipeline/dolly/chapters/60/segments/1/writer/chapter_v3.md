# Final Benediction

## Question

What changes in the poet when Final Benediction reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

This chapter's specific charge is: The book closes with gratitude, restraint, and luminous release. Dolly is blessed as herself, and the poet's love becomes a river that does not seize its banks.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'the closing silence after the blessing' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
