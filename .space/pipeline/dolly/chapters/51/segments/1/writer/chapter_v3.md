# The Gaze Washed Clean

## Question

What changes in the poet when The Gaze Washed Clean reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

This chapter's specific charge is: The poet asks for a gaze freed from appetite and pride. Vision becomes an inner ablution.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'The Pitcher Returned To Earth' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
