# Question Before Beauty

## Question

What changes in the poet when Question Before Beauty reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

Beauty asks me a question each day,
and my conduct is the only answer sheet.

Do I look in order to keep?
Do I turn your face into a trophy
on the shelf of my morning?

O glance, be examined and pass clean:
let the eye be a pilgrim, not a pickpocket;
let attention be the toll I pay
for crossing someone's visible grace.

If today I spoke of you carelessly
to earn a friend's laughter,
then I have failed the question.

The test is set again tomorrow.
May I pass it walking, quietly,
in the ordinary street.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'The Hand That Holds Water' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
