# Radha's Shadow, Dolly's Face

## Question

What changes in the poet when Radha's Shadow, Dolly's Face reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

Again the lazy symbol came to hand:
Goddess, moon, river, lotus —
the secondhand ornaments of poetry.

And her face declined them all.

It said: I am this exact face,
with this small asymmetry,
this weather of moods, this laughter
that arrives a half-beat late.

The resemblance to Radha survives
only because the person is real;
symbol is purified by attention,
the way gold is by fire.

So correct me, Dolly, each time
my ink reaches for the generic crown.
Real faces are harder to sing
than myths — and truer.

Let every lazy symbol
stand embarrassed in your light,
unemployed, learning
the discipline of the particular.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'The Poet's Shame' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
