# The Poet's Shame

## Question

What changes in the poet when The Poet's Shame reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

Yes — I admit it: some of my praise
was my own face in a mirror,
admiration circling back to the admirer,
vanity borrowing devotion's white cloth.

Shame arrived, and let it stay —
not as the final defeat
but as the dawn's cold water.

Shame says: you sang to be seen singing.
Shame says: the poem was your temple
and you its only idol.

Washed, I return to the page.
The difference is small and total:
now I write facing away from myself,
toward her actual evening,
her actual city, her freedom —
and if the verse is clean now,
the credit belongs to the burning
that removed me from it.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'Water At The Edge Of Speech' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
