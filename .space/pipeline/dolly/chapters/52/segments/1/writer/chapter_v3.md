# The Pitcher Returned To Earth

## Question

What changes in the poet when The Pitcher Returned To Earth reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

This chapter's specific charge is: Clay returns the poem to mortality and gratitude. The sacred object is honored as earth before it becomes symbol.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'Dolly's Silence' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
