# Distance As Devotion

## Question

What changes in the poet when Distance As Devotion reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

I have learned to bless by stepping back —
the way the heron lifts from the shallows
so the water can settle and show
what it was hiding all along.

Distance is not coldness, Dolly.
It is the shade I extend over your door,
the half-turn of my face at the crossing,
the letter written and left unwritten.

The gardener who loves the rose
does not camp inside its petals.
He keeps the ground moist, the fence mended,
and his praise silent as dew.

May my reverence have this far strategy:
near enough to help, far enough to free,
so that you never once mistake
my careful love for a rope.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'Dolly In The Market Light' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
