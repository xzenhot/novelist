# Dolly's Silence

## Question

What changes in the poet when Dolly's Silence reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

This chapter's specific charge is: Dolly's silence is honored as meaning, privacy, and freedom. The poet learns not to force an answer from what is complete without him.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'The Poet's Inner Vrindavan' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
