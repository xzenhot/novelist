# Dolly's Human Radiance

## Question

What changes in the poet when Dolly's Human Radiance reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

Enough of rivers. Look at the person:
she argues with the auto-wallah over thirty rupees,
calls her mother on the metro,
buys coriander, laughs at her phone.

Her radiance is not mythology.
It is the ordinary self-possession
of a woman carrying her own life
through a city of nine million strangers.

Forgive me, Dolly, for the episodes
in which I nearly embalmed you in light.
A beloved is not a metaphor with a pulse.

Reverence begins here:
you are free, contemporary, unowned,
and the wonder is that you are real.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'The Yamuna Within' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
