# The Clay Maker's Lesson

## Question

What changes in the poet when The Clay Maker's Lesson reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

Watch the potter at his wheel:
thumbs reading clay like scripture,
pressure timed to the vessel's breath.

Too little touch, and the wall collapses.
Too much, and the clay forgets its shape.
The pot exists between force and release.

So too a life, Dolly; so too a song.
The beloved is never made by the maker's
insistence, only by disciplined hands
that know when to steady and when to withdraw.

I would be shaped like that —
useful, round, holding water without complaint —
and I would praise you that way:
touch applied exactly,
purpose ruling the fingers,
and then, the wheel still humming,
the hands letting go.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'Monsoon Over New Delhi' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
