# Prayer Without Temple

## Question

What changes in the poet when Prayer Without Temple reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

No temple required. Devotion has moved
into streets, rooms, thresholds —
a portable sanctuary, unsanctioned,
unannounced by any bell.

The doorway where I pause each evening
is a shrine of restraint.
The stairwell is my ghat;
each step, a small descent of pride.

In the market a woman once
poured water to a thirsty crow
and the whole pavement turned to Vraja.

So I carry my worship folded
like a handkerchief in the pocket —
available, discreet,
unneeded by its subject.

The sacred needs no committee here.
It rides the bus, pays in coins,
and gets off, quietly,
two stops before anyone notices.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'Radha's Shadow, Dolly's Face' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
