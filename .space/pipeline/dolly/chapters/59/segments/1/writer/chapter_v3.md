# Song Without Possession

## Question

What changes in the poet when Song Without Possession reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

This chapter's specific charge is: The poet's final art is praise without ownership. Song remains, but its hunger has been clarified into gratitude.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'Final Benediction' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
