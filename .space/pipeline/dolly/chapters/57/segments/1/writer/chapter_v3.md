# Yamuna Of The Heart

## Question

What changes in the poet when Yamuna Of The Heart reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

This chapter's specific charge is: The river fully enters the poet's conscience. Inner water becomes memory, devotion, and self-command.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'Dolly Free In Delhi' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
