# Beauty And Responsibility

## Question

What changes in the poet when Beauty And Responsibility reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

Beauty hands me a receipt:
now you owe care.

To have seen truly
is to be enrolled —
in truth-telling, in restraint,
in the protection of what I praised.

Admiration without accountability
is tourism. I refuse to be
a tourist of a human face.

So let my witness be audited:
Do I speak of her as she is,
or as my song requires?
Do I defend her quiet
when others speculate?
Does my looking leave her
exactly as free as before?

Beauty is not a favor done to my eyes.
It is a trust transferred.
O gift, I sign for you —
in care, in truth, in restraint,
payable daily, in conduct.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'The Clay Maker's Lesson' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
