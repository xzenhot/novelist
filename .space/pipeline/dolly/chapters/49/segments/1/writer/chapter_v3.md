# The Garland Refused

## Question

What changes in the poet when The Garland Refused reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

I wanted to garland you with verses,
hang my best adjectives on your morning,
plant my small flag of praise.

You passed by, unsurpassed,
needing nothing from my language,
and the garland stayed in my hands
like a lamp someone declined.

Refusal became my instruction:
the beloved does not require
the poet's decoration to be whole.
The jasmine does not need the poem.
The river does not need the bank's opinion.

So I hold the unworn garland gratefully.
Its flowers still open; they will feed
some other evening, some humbler shrine.

And you walk ahead, complete, unadorned,
teaching the oldest lesson of beauty:
it arrives; it does not apply.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'Sacred Ordinary Morning' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
