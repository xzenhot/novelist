# Dolly Beyond Comparison

## Question

What changes in the poet when Dolly Beyond Comparison reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

Now put the comparison down.
Not Radha's sister, not the river's echo,
not a theme with a beautiful face.

Dolly, singular, unpaired, whole —
her laughter filed under no legend,
her name entering no genealogy.

Love matures the day resemblance yields
to attention: when I stop seeing
a myth that happens to walk
and start seeing you walking.

You are not like anything.
That is your freedom and my lesson.
Even the Yamuna must forgive me
for ranking you beneath her silence.

The map ends where the person begins.
Welcome, plain and unprecedented,
to the only country called you.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'Yamuna At Night' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
