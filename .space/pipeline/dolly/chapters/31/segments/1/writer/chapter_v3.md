# The River's Polluted Mirror

## Question

What changes in the poet when The River's Polluted Mirror reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

I wanted a pure river for my metaphor.
The Yamuna offers me foam and stench,
black water carrying the city's refusal.

Devotion, meet grief. Grief, devotion.
You will have to share the ghat.

How can I sing river-goddess
with sewage in my mouth?
And how can I not —
when even wounded, the river flows;
even insulted, she carries
the lamps floated on her by faith?

So let my song be desecrated
the way the water is:
honest, grieving, still devoted.

Nostalgia is cheap; care is costly.
I will not escape to a mythical past river.
I will love this one —
foam, filth, faith, and all —
the way one loves the real beloved:
in her actual, damaged, holy present.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'Beauty And Responsibility' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
