# Yamuna At Night

## Question

What changes in the poet when Yamuna At Night reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

At night the river keeps its own counsel.
No vendor, no pilgrim, no singer —
only water moving in the dark
with its cargo of unsaid things.

Night water is a figure for prayer:
depth you cannot audit,
currents you trust without proof,
a surface that returns the moon
whether you deserve it or not.

I stand on the bridge at midnight, Dolly,
not following you — following the silence
your name left in my day.

Some knowledge is only given
to those who stop demanding it.
The river does not explain itself.
Neither will you. That is well.
I ask only to stand near the depth,
unarmed, and listening.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'The City As Witness' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
