# The Poet's Inner Vrindavan

## Question

What changes in the poet when The Poet's Inner Vrindavan reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

This chapter's specific charge is: Vrindavan becomes an inward field of disciplined love. The poem moves from geography to conscience without losing earthly detail.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'A Blessing At The Riverbank' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
