# Dolly In The Market Light

## Question

What changes in the poet when Dolly In The Market Light reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

Sabzi Mandi at seven: coriander
bundled like green hymns, tomatoes
keeping their small red faith,
coins counted into a cracked palm.

In the middle of commerce, revelation —
the vendor's blessing with the extra chilli,
the scale that does not cheat,
cloth folded for a poor customer
with both hands, like an offering.

You bought spinach there yesterday.
The transaction was three minutes long.
And yet the whole market keeps
the shape of your passing, the way
a river keeps the shape of boats.

Ordinary trade, extraordinary grace.
Devotion hides in the ledger too.
I bought coriander and stood still,
richer than the day's receipts could say.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'A Dupatta Like Water' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
