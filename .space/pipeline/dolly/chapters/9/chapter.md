# The Poet's Restlessness

## Question

What changes in the poet when The Poet's Restlessness reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

Be honest, poet: part of this song is appetite.
You dressed a glance as darsan,
called your hunger the river's pull.

The city sees through you —
the beggar, the auto-wallah, the moon,
all of them unimpressed by your metaphors.

Good. Let the restlessness burn its residue.
A gaze that has never confessed
cannot be trusted with a face.

So I confess: I wanted, and called it worship.
Only then, washed, do I lift the lamp again —
smaller now, steadier,
a light that has apologized to its own flame.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'Dolly's Human Radiance' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
