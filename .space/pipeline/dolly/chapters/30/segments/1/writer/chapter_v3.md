# Evening Lamps Of Delhi

## Question

What changes in the poet when Evening Lamps Of Delhi reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

The window above the tailor's shop
switches on its single bulb:
benediction, forty watts.

The dhaba's hanging lamp
swings a small halo over the tea;
the street-cricket match plays on
under borrowed floodlight;
a night-flower opens in a windowsill pot
for no audience at all.

Devotion has many wattages.
Some are domestic and dim —
the mother's stove burning past midnight,
the student's desk-lamp, the shrine
in the watchman's wooden box.

The city carries these lights
like a pitcher carried carefully:
not to be spilled, not to be seen.

And every evening they bless
the passerby who did not ask,
including me, including my song —
small benedictions, moving through Delhi,
unpaid, unnoticed, unfailing.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'The River's Polluted Mirror' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
