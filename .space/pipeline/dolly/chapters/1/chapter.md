# First Sight In New Delhi

## Question

What changes in the poet when First Sight In New Delhi reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

On a Delhi balcony the evening begins its aarti —
one bulb, one crow returning, one kettle's breath.
Then you cross the lane below, and the city
forgets its hurry, the way a river forgets rain.

I do not reach for you. Recognition
is a lamp that lights itself
when the right face passes the door —
and behind your face, faint as flute-sound under traffic,
old Yamuna turns in her sleep.

Carry your pitcher, sister of the river-light;
I will stand here, quiet, glad,
that a human face can wake a sacred map
without once being asked to be a goddess.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'Radha By The Yamuna' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
