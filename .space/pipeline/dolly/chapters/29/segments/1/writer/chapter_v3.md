# A Name Written In Air

## Question

What changes in the poet when A Name Written In Air reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

Dolly — even the name is a breath:
the tongue touching, releasing,
nothing harsh in it anywhere.

An invocation, but also a boundary:
to speak it is to cross
onto someone's private land,
so I speak it sparingly,
like matches in monsoon.

How is a name cherished
without being consumed?

By becoming prayer instead of summons:
said once in the morning
to tune the heart's veena,
then folded away;
never said to call her near,
always said to wish her well
wherever the sound cannot reach.

Let the name remain hers.
I will keep only the breath —
returned, each night, unspent.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'Evening Lamps Of Delhi' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
