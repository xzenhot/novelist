# A River Under Asphalt

## Question

What changes in the poet when A River Under Asphalt reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

Under the asphalt, an old riverbed;
under the riverbed, Tethys salt;
under the salt, the memory of water
that no municipality can revoke.

Ancient feeling behaves the same way.
It waits beneath the commute,
beneath the salary, beneath the jokes —
buried water in the poet's bones.

At the metro station, one second
between two trains: the flood returns.
I hear a bhav gaan in the announcement's crackle.
I smell khal-water in the tunnel draft.

The city paved the rivers and the songs.
It did not reach the water table.

So I dig, Dolly, in broad daylight,
singing low —
and when my spade rings stone,
I keep digging. Water remembers us.
That is enough to build a faith on.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'The Poet's Jealousy' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
