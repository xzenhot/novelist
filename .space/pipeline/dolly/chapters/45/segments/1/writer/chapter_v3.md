# The Beloved's Laughter

## Question

What changes in the poet when The Beloved's Laughter reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

Then, over the market's gray shoulder —
your laughter. Unplanned, unsolemn,
cracking the whole morning open
like a window in a shut room.

Reverence had grown heavy on me;
I was becoming a museum of respect.
Your laughter came and opened the doors,
aired the curtains of my seriousness.

Joy is not the enemy of devotion.
It is devotion released from its posture.
Even Krishna's flute was play:
the holy refusing to be solemn.

Laugh, Dolly, at the world and at my verses.
I would rather be the reason for one
honest laugh than for a thousand
perfect, dead elegies.
Lightness, too, is a form of praise.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'The Weight Of The Pitcher' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
