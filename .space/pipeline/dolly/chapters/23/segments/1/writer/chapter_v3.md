# Delhi Heat, Yamuna Memory

## Question

What changes in the poet when Delhi Heat, Yamuna Memory reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

June presses the capital flat;
tar softens, tempers shorten,
the city will not slow down for anyone's thirst.

And longing becomes literal:
a dryness with my name on it,
memory turning into shade —
the banyan at the bus stand,
the awning of a chai-shop,
anywhere her remembered coolness pools.

I walk looking for shade
and keep finding her instead:
under the neem, a posture; in the shade,
a turn of head; the water-seller's cart
shouting its kindness through the heat.

O summer, you are honest at least:
you admit that love is thirst,
and thirst keeps walking —
through a city that never slows,
toward a coolness it cannot bottle.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'The Flute Not Heard' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
