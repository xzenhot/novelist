# Monsoon Over New Delhi

## Question

What changes in the poet when Monsoon Over New Delhi reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

The first shower hits the kikar trees
and the whole city exhales at once —
dust laid low, tar made sweet,
children running where the drains divide.

Rain tests my memory of the river:
does the Yamuna still exist
beyond this chemical smell of wet road?
The monsoon promises it so.

And through the rinsed evening you passed,
hair damp, sandals in your hand,
your image moving through the cleaned air
like a fish through newly honest water.

Renew the city, rain; renew the river;
renew in me the plain belief
that what is washed can be loved
without being kept.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'Distance As Devotion' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
