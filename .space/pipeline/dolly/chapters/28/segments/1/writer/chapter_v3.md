# Water At The Edge Of Speech

## Question

What changes in the poet when Water At The Edge Of Speech reveals Dolly not as an object of possession, but as a living threshold between New Delhi and the remembered Yamuna?

## Oration

Just before language there is a gathering —
a stillness on the tongue's edge,
the feeling standing at the door
with its shoes off.

Most of my truest feelings
never made it into sentences.
They stayed in the anteroom,
complete, unruined by nouns.

I have said her name badly
on the very days I loved her best;
and on shallow days, spoken well.

So now I let some things
remain pre-verbal: the ache at 6 p.m.,
the gratitude with no object,
the prayer that is only posture.

O threshold of speech, keep them.
What has not been said
cannot be mis-said.
Some emotions are truest
in their unfinished state —
like water still in the cloud.

## Benediction

Let the closing blessing leave Dolly free, the poet humbled, and the reader carried toward 'A Name Written In Air' with a quieter, cleaner gaze.
