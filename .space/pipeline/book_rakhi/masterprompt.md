# Master Prompt - Rakhi: The Thread of Memory

## Book Identity

- **Book name:** rakhi
- **Long title:** Rakhi: The Thread of Memory
- **Genre:** Family Drama / Literary Fiction
- **Language:** English
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Target audience:** general readers of literary family drama

## Central Premise

A family drama about memory, duty, and a sister's promise.

## Chapter Plan

- Chapter 1: The Thread Kept In A Tin Box - The story opens with Rakhi returning to her childhood home after years away, finding an old ceremonial thread and a letter that recalls a promise made to her brother before the family broke apart.
- Chapter 2: Mother's Locked Room - Rakhi enters her late mother's locked room and discovers fragments of a family history that everyone else has learned to avoid.
- Chapter 3: The Brother Who Stayed - Arjun, the brother who remained behind, carries resentment and duty in equal measure while the household quietly depends on his sacrifices.
- Chapter 4: A Promise Before The Monsoon - A flashback reveals the childhood promise Rakhi made during a difficult monsoon, when she swore to protect the family from a truth she barely understood.
- Chapter 5: The Ledger Of Duties - Money, inheritance, and unpaid care work surface as family members argue over what duty means and who has paid its price.
- Chapter 6: Festival Preparations - As the family prepares for the Raksha Bandhan ceremony, rituals revive tenderness but also expose the wounds hidden beneath politeness.
- Chapter 7: The Missing Photograph - A missing photograph points toward a buried conflict involving their father, an old debt, and a decision that changed the siblings' lives.
- Chapter 8: Nila's Version - Nila challenges Rakhi's memory of the past and forces her to see that love can preserve truth and distort it at the same time.
- Chapter 9: The Aunt Who Remembered - An elderly aunt reveals a half-truth about the family's old rupture, deepening the mystery around the sister's promise.
- Chapter 10: What The Thread Could Not Hold - The ceremonial thread becomes a symbol of protection and pressure as Rakhi realizes that promises can bind people together or keep them trapped.
- Chapter 11: Arjun's Anger - Arjun finally speaks of the years he lost caring for others, and the siblings confront the unequal shape of family obligation.
- Chapter 12: The Letter Unsent - Rakhi finds an unsent letter from her mother that reframes the family's silence as protection, fear, and regret.
- Chapter 13: The Day Father Left - The novel returns to the decisive day their father left, revealing how a child's understanding hardened into an adult's burden.
- Chapter 14: Debt And Forgiveness - Old financial and emotional debts are counted, but the family begins to ask whether forgiveness can exist without full repayment.
- Chapter 15: The Ceremony Breaks - During the Raksha Bandhan ceremony, a public accusation shatters the fragile peace and forces the hidden truth into the open.
- Chapter 16: Rakhi's Confession - Rakhi confesses what she promised, what she concealed, and why she stayed away, risking the last trust her siblings still have in her.
- Chapter 17: The Brother's Choice - Arjun must decide whether duty will remain a chain or become an act chosen freely after the truth has been spoken.
- Chapter 18: Repairing The House - The family begins literal and emotional repairs, discovering that reconciliation is ordinary work rather than a single dramatic forgiveness.
- Chapter 19: A New Thread - A new ceremony is prepared, not to repeat the past but to redefine protection as mutual honesty and shared responsibility.
- Chapter 20: The Promise Released - Rakhi releases the childhood promise and makes an adult one: to remember truthfully, love without control, and let duty become care.

## Core Characters

- Rakhi Sen: Elder sister returning home and keeper of the central promise
- Arjun Sen: Younger brother who stayed behind to carry family duties
- Mira Sen: Late mother whose letters and silences shape the plot
- Nila Sen: Younger sister who challenges the family's accepted memories
- Dev Sen: Absent father whose choices created the family's central wound

## Style Mandate

Write as literary family drama: emotionally precise, scene-driven, observant about memory and duty, and grounded in ordinary domestic detail. Let objects, rooms, rituals, letters, silences, and repeated gestures carry emotional weight.

## Three-Section Structure

Every chapter preserves three sections:

1. **Workshop (frame)** - the modern scene where characters discuss. Keep unchanged when rewriting.
2. **Story (narrative)** - the narrated story. Rewrite richly to the target length.
3. **Discussion (frame)** - the characters' reactions. Keep unchanged when rewriting.
