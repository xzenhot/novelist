## Grounding Notes
- Ground the narrative in the traditional life of Siddhartha Gautama (c. 563–483 BCE).
- Weave the tension between the sheltered life and the reality of suffering into the story.
- Emphasize the question "why do we suffer, and can suffering be ended?" as the story's spine.
- End with the hope that the teaching, once kindled, is never extinguished.
