## Subject

The life and teaching of **Siddhartha Gautama** (c. 563–483 BCE), the prince of the Shakya clan who became the **Buddha** — the Awakened One — and whose teaching transformed human living and reinvented philosophy.

## Era and Place

- **Era:** Ancient India, c. 5th–4th century BCE (the traditional dating of the Buddha's life).
- **Places:** Kapilavastu (birth and palace), the Ganges plain, Bodh Gaya (enlightenment), Sarnath (first sermon), Kushinagar (parinirvana).

## Key Figures

| Figure | Role |
|--------|------|
| Siddhartha Gautama | The prince who becomes the Buddha |
| King Suddhodana | His father, who shields him from suffering |
| Mara | The tempter — desire, fear, and delusion |
| The Five Companions | The first disciples |

## Key Events (per chapter)

1. **The Sheltered Prince** — the prophecy at birth, the palace of pleasure, the Four Sights (an old man, a sick man, a corpse, a wandering ascetic).
2. **The Great Renunciation** — the leaving in the night, the years of study and austerity, the Middle Way.
3. **The Awakening** — the night under the Bodhi tree, the assault of Mara, the Enlightenment, the First Sermon at Sarnath.
4. **The Teaching** — the forty-five years of teaching, the founding of the Sangha, the spread of the teaching.
5. **The Echo** — the final teaching at Kushinagar, the parinirvana, what remains.

## Core Doctrines

- **The Four Noble Truths** — the truth of suffering (dukkha), its cause (craving), its cessation, and the path to its end.
- **The Eightfold Path** — right view, right intention, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, right concentration.
- **Anatta** (no fixed self) and **anicca** (impermanence).
- **The Middle Way** — the path between indulgence and self-mortification.
- **Karuna** (compassion) and **metta** (loving-kindness).

## Grounding Notes

- The narrative is grounded in the traditional accounts of the Buddha's life, not a documentary history. The correctness bar is attributional and internal-consistency, not documentary.
- The Four Sights, the Great Renunciation, the Middle Way, the Enlightenment, and the First Sermon are the traditional pillars of the Buddha's biography.
- The doctrines (Four Noble Truths, Eightfold Path, anatta, anicca) are correctly attributed to the Buddha's teaching.
