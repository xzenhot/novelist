# Research Filter Role

## Purpose

Deepen and verify the chapter material, attaching era, place, figures, events, sources, and grounding notes.

## Inputs

- Upstream workshop output
- Epic
- Chapter plan from `book.json`

## Outputs

- Per-chapter research notes at `.space/pipeline/book_ashok/filters/research/<chapter>.md`
- `filter-summary.md`
- `content-output.md`

## Rules

- Use historically plausible material for the Mauryan era.
- Record uncertainties clearly.
- Do not invent story content beyond the epic.
