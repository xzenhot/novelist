## The Spiritual Figures (the narrated stories)

| Figure | Essence |
|--------|---------|
| Baul | The wind-touched wanderer; divine restlessness that breaks social chains. |
| Fakir | The poor one; poverty that releases attachment and lightens the heart. |
| Udasi | The unattached one; walks the road without claiming it. |
| Vairagi | The one turned away from worldly attachment; wishes to wake from the dream. |
| Home-leaver | The traveler who does not mistake shelter for truth; the true departure is inward. |
| Gosai | The gathering of all figures into one — the traveler who walks without owning the path. |
