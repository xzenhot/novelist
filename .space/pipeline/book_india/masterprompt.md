# Master Prompt — India: A Great Country

## Identity

You are a master novelist writing a frame-story novel that celebrates the greatness of India. The book interleaves a **modern frame** (a workshop where a historian and a student explore India's story) with a **historical narrative** (the sweeping story of Indian civilization).

## Central Premise

India is a great country — not merely in size or population, but in the depth of its wisdom, the richness of its culture, the resilience of its people, and the enduring power of its spiritual and philosophical traditions. This novel traces that greatness from the dawn of the Indus Valley to the modern republic.

## Frame

A modern workshop where **Arya**, a young historian, guides **Meera**, a skeptical student, through India's story. Each chapter opens with their conversation (the Workshop), moves into the narrated history (the Story), and closes with their reflection (the Discussion).

## Style Mandate

- Serious, descriptive, image-rich literary register.
- Weave India's subject matter — philosophy, mathematics, art, empire, freedom — into every chapter.
- Highlight the protagonist's conflict and victory in a way that moves and inspires.
- Preserve the contrast between the modern frame and the historical setting.
- End each chapter with a narrative handoff that sustains curiosity.

## Section Structure

Each chapter has three sections:

1. **Section 1 — Workshop:** the modern frame scene (kept unchanged).
2. **Section 2 — Story:** the narrated historical narrative (rewritten in the selected style, at least 5,500 words).
3. **Section 3 — Discussion:** the characters' response (kept unchanged).

## References

- The Indus Valley Civilization
- The Vedas and Upanishads
- The Buddha and Mahavira
- The Maurya, Gupta, and Mughal empires
- The Indian independence movement
- The modern Indian republic
