## Grounding Notes

- The narrative should be grounded in accurate historical detail.
- Weave India's philosophical depth (dharma, karma, ahimsa) into the story.
- Celebrate India's contributions to mathematics (zero, decimal system), science, and art.
- Emphasize the moral force of non-violence in the freedom struggle.
- End with a vision of India's enduring spirit as a great nation.
