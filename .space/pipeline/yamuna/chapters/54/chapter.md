# The Breath Beneath the Foam

## Question

Is there breath under all the foam? Even in the worst of my illness, there is still a river alive beneath the scum — a flow that persists, a chemistry that can recover, a current that has not yet given up. What does that hidden breath promise a city at the end of its patience?

## Oration

Look past the foam and you will still see it: the current moving beneath the layer, the thread of water that has not surrendered. I am not only what floats on my surface; I am that deeper useless that the foam only covers — the moving mass that has persisted through every assault and still answers gravity. That breath is my proof that the sickness has not yet turned to death. Because there is still movement, there is still the chance of return; the water that can be cleaned is a water that can meet the river again.

The foam is a description of the present; the breath below is a promise for the future. A city that sees only the scum will despair and give up; a city that learns to look past the scum, to watch the moving thread that has not ceased, will find the will to act at last. My foam is the wound, but the breath beneath it is the flesh that can heal. Find that breath; keep it; follow it. For it persists exactly as long as people are willing to look beneath the surface — and no river is beyond saving while the thread of it is still moving, alive, and reaching.

## Benediction

Let the river's breath be the object of the city's love, beneath the foam that hides it. Let the persistence of the current outlast the despair. And may the breath that remains under the gross lift at last to the surface — a river that has come up for air, whole and clean.
