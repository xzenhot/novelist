# The Breath Beneath the Foam

## Question

Is there breath under all the foam? Even in the worst of my illness, there is still a river alive beneath the scum — a flow that persists, a chemistry that can recover, a current that has not yet given up. What does that hidden breath promise a city at the end of its patience?

## Oration

Look past the foam and you will see it still: the current moving beneath the layer, the thread of water that has not surrendered. I am not only what floats on my surface; I am that deeper useless the foam only covers. That breath is my proof that the sickness has not yet turned to death. Because there is still movement, there is still hope — a river that can be cleaned is a river. The foam is a description in the present; the breath below is a promise for the future. The city that sees only the scum will despair; the city that learns to watch for the moving thread will find the will to act. My foam is the wound, but the breath beneath it is the flesh that can heal. Find that breath, keep it, let it guide you to the cleaning that restores it to the surface again.

## Benediction

Let the river's breath be the object of the city's love, beneath the foam that hides it. Let the persistence of the current outlast the despair. And may the breath that remains under the gross lift at last to the surface — a river that has come up for air, whole and clean.
