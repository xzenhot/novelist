# The River in the Kitchen

## Question

When did the river leave the kitchen? There was a time when water came to the threshold by its own path — drawn, poured, felt, known in every household. Now the tap opens and water arrives without origin, without face. What does a kitchen that cannot remember its river lose?

## Oration

Water moves through the kitchen like an unremembered guest. The pot on the stove once held what a whole river had traveled a whole country to bring; the hands that reached for it did not know the length of that journey. I am the great water at the source that becomes the small water in the glass. When a city knows this — that every sip began far upstream, in snow, in cloud, in the body of the river — then the kitchen becomes a shrine of remembrance. A household that can name its river is harder to make indifferent. Ask of every drop: where has this traveled, and who will see it again? The night snipe the pot, and in that snip is the whole covenant of the basin. A river that lives in the kitchen is a river that will be remembered.

## Benediction

Let the river be at home in the kitchen, named and known. Let the glass remember the long road the water traveled. And may every meal made from the piped river be a quiet act of gratitude for the river that made the city.
