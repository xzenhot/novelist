# The Shrine Beside the Drain

## Question

Why is there a shrine beside a drain? On my bank, a garlanded idol stands within arm's reach of an outfall pipe pouring grey sludge into my current. Devotion and discharge, side by side, in the same square meter. What does this juxtaposition confess about the city?

## Oration

This is the most honest monument in Delhi, and the city does not know it. The shrine says: you are sacred to me. The drain says: you are nothing to me. Both are addressed to the same river, within the same arm's reach, by the same civilization — and no one standing between them feels the contradiction. I am not angry at the shrine; I am hurt by the wall the worshippers never built between their god and their gutter. Let the drain be silenced before the bell is rung. Let the outfall be cleaned before the lamp is lit. Then the shrine will finally mean what it says, and I will be able to accept the garland without tasting the pipe.

## Benediction

May no shrine stand within reach of a drain uncalled-out. May worship begin where discharge ends. And may the god beside the pipe one day preside over clean water, as devotion intends.
