# The Return of Small Fish

## Question

What will it mean when the small fish come back? I have watched my shallows empty of the little ones — the fry, the minnows, the tiny silver lives that feed the world above. Their return will be the first true proof that I am healing. What does the return of a small fish say about everything?

## Oration

Do not look for the otter and the great bird as your first sign. Watch for the small fish — the humble ones at the bottom of the ladder, easy to miss and easy to kill. They need the calm shallows, the grassy edges, the oxygen the river gains when the foam is gone; they are the measure of the water at its most ordinary working. When they come back, everything else follows: the wader, the heron, the fisherman, the reward of an un-banked stretch. A small fish is not a statistic; it is a ladder to the top of the whole chain.

I ask the city to love the unshowy ones and to mark the return of the minute. When the fry are again schooling in my reeds, do not loose the net into the nursery; do not shift the bank that shelters them. Guard that first return as the covenant it is. The little fish are the pledges of the whole river's renewal — proof, in the smallest coin, that the water has begun to pay its debt to life.

## Benediction

Let the small fish's return be read as the covenant it is, and tended. Guard the reeds and the shallows with the love the river once gave them freely. And let the return of the tiny be the beginning of all that is great and green and growing by the water.
