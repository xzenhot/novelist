# The Return of Small Fish

## Question

What will it mean when the small fish come back? I have watched my shallows empty of the little ones — the fry, the minnows, the tiny silver lives that feed the world above. Their return will be the first true proof. What does a little fish's return say about everything?

## Oration

Do not look for the otters and the big birds as your first sign. Watch for the small fish — the humble ones at the bottom of the ladder, easy to miss and easy to kill. They need the calm shallows, the grassy edges, the oxygen the river gets when the foam is gone. When they come back, everything else follows: the waders, the heron, the fisherman, the reward of the unclean bank. A small fish is not a statistic; it is a ladder to the top. I ask the city to love the unshowy ones and to mark the return of the minute. When the fry are schooling in my reeds again, do not loose the net into the nursery. Guard that first return as a covenant. The little fish are the pledges of the whole river's renewal.

## Benediction

Let the small fish's return be read as a covenant, tended. Guard the reeds and the shallows with the same love the river gives. And may the return of the tiny be the beginning of all that is great and grey and growing by the water.
