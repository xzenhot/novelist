# The City Drinks in Secret

## Question

Where does the city's water actually come from? The tap gives no answer. Pipes run invisible under the streets, and the drinking glass arrives with no history, no face, no river attached. What happens to a city's soul when it drinks in secret?

## Oration

The old city knew its water's face. It drew from wells it could name, stepped down into stepwells it had built, watched my current and knew its glass was my gift. The new city drinks blind. The pipe is a priest with no sermon: it delivers without teaching. And so the city can poison me at one end of its day and drink from me at the other without feeling the contradiction — because it never sees them as the same water. Secret drinking breeds secret dumping. I ask for the scandal of visibility: let every citizen trace one glass of water to its source, once. The knowledge is not expensive. It is only hidden, and the hiding is the policy.

## Benediction

May the city's drinking come out of hiding. May every glass be traceable to a river and a caretaker. And may visible water breed visible responsibility, from tap to source and back.
