# Delhi's Thirsting Throat

## Question

How much can a city drink before it drinks itself? Delhi's thirst grows with every census — and every liter is drawn from my diminishing flow, my deeper wells, my shrinking share. When the city's throat is this large, who remembers the water has a throat too?

## Oration

I have fed this city from a hamlet to a giant, and I am proud of the feeding. But hear the arithmetic of thirst: the metropolis grows daily, my share does not. Upstream states take their portions, the aquifer falls, and the demand curve climbs like a smoke line. A throat this large needs more than my body can spare — unless the city changes what swallowing means. Reuse what you use. Harvest what falls from your sky. Stop sending my water back as poison so that fresh water must be fetched to replace it. The thirst is legitimate; the waste is not. I am not the limit of your ambition. I am the limit of your apology.

## Benediction

May the great throat learn to drink without wasting. May every drop drawn be matched by a drop returned clean. And may the city's growth be measured not only in towers but in teaspoons of care.
