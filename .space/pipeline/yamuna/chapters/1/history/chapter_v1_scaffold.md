# Before the City Had a Name

## Question

Who gave the first drink? Before Delhi had a name, a slow grey river crossed the plain, and the animals came down at dusk to drink. When the first humans knelt beside them, did they know the water that cooled their tongues would remember them longer than any stone they would ever raise?

## Oration

The river speaks as the eldest witness: I was here before the city, before the naming. I gave water to deer and tiger, to the first clay pots, to the first fires lit on my banks. My current was young then, my floodplain wide and green, my chest unburdened. The people who drank from me carried me in their gratitude — no temple yet, only the instinct to bow at the water's edge. I was not holy because anyone said so; I was holy the way a mother is, by having given. Remember this beginning, city: you were not built beside me. You were raised by me.

## Benediction

May the city remember it was weaned on this water. May every drink taken today carry a faint memory of that first kneeling. And may the river that came before the name outlast the name, still giving, still asking to be thanked.
