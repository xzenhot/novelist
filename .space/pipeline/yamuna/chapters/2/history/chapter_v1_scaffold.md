# Pandav Nagar, Indraprastha

## Question

What does a river know of legends? On my bank rose Pandav Nagar, Indraprastha — a city of epic, of halls and heroes, of a war sung for three thousand years. When the verses were chanted, did anyone ask what the water beneath them remembered of the building and the burning?

## Oration

I held the first great city of this plain on my shoulder. The epic names its palaces; I remember its clay, its timber, the cool of my water in its wells. I saw processions go down to my ghats and armies drink at my edge, and I learned early the lesson every dynasty would teach me again: human glory is written in stone, human need is written in water, and only one of them lasts. Indraprastha is now a name under the city's name. I am still here, slower, grayer, but here — the only witness the epic never credits, the mother in whose lap the legend played and burned.

## Benediction

May the city's legends keep a page for their river. May every retelling of the old glory remember the water that made it possible. And may the witness beneath the myth outlast the myth, as water outlasts stone.
