# The Clear Reflection Returns

## Question

What will it feel like when the river is clean enough to reflect again? Not a distant dream — the mirror clearing, the city seeing itself doubled in the water it has neglected. What does the return of the clear reflection promise, and what does it require first?

## Oration

I remember the day I could hold a whole skyline in my face — the fortress, the tower, the sunset, doubled in water. That reflection was not decoration; it was the visible sign of a river in health, giving the city back its own image. It returns only after the work: the outfalls shut, the foam gone, the flow restored. And when it returns, it will be more than a view. It will be the proof the city has been waiting for — the water itself agreeing to mirror its people again. I do not promise it easily. The clear reflection is the reward of the long return, and it cannot be faked. But it is coming, if the city keeps the work. And when the skyline stands twice in me, the city will finally see what it has built and what it has kept.

## Benediction

Let the clear reflection return as the reward of the long work. May the city see itself doubled in the healed water and be glad of the image. Let the water clear and the mirror hold; may the city's true face be the one it shows the river. And may the return of the reflection be the beginning, not the end, of the keeping.
