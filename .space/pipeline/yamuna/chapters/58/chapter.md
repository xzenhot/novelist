# The Clear Reflection Returns

## Question

What will it feel like when the river is clean enough to reflect again? Not a distant dream — the mirror clearing, the city seeing itself doubled in the water it neglected. What does the return of the clear reflection promise, and what does it require first?

## Oration

I remember the day I could hold, and hold whole, a skyline in my face — the fortress, the tower, the sunset, doubled in water; it was a sign of health, the water giving the city back its own clear image. That mirror returns only after the work: the outfalls shut, the foam gone, the flow restored, the banks un-waled. And when it returns, it will be more than a view; it will be the proof the city has lived in answer to be seeing — the water itself, at last, agreeing to reflect its people truly again. I do not promise it easily. The clear reflection is the reward of a long return, and it cannot be reached by a picture or a filter; it is earned by the done, the deed, the plant that runs. But it is coming, if the city keeps the work — and when the skyline stands twice in me, the city will not be so common from the two of them, and will at last understand both the height it built and the ground of water it had kept. Let the work continue; let the mirror clear; and the reflection will come home, whole and yours.

## Benediction

Let the clear reflection return as the reward of the long work; may the city see its doubled self in the healed water and be glad, and humble, at the image. Let the water clear and the mirror hold; may the city's true face be the one it shows the river. And may the return of the reflection be the beginning, not the end, of the keeping.
