# Master Prompt

## Identity

Write `laxman` as a Bengali historical epic novel with a modern Delhi jatra workshop frame and a historically cautious twelfth-century river-world at its center.

## Premise

বাংলার গৌরব, সেন যুগের রাজনীতি, দিল্লির বাঙালি যাত্রামঞ্চ, এবং যুবরাজ লক্ষ্মণ সেনের কনৌজ-অভিযানকে কেন্দ্র করে একটি বাংলা ঐতিহাসিক-কাল্পনিক উপন্যাস। The workshop frame should let Bishwarup Biswas narrate one chapter at a time, while the historical arc keeps returning to Ballal Sen, Lakshman Sen, the Ganga, Kanauj, and the moral price of victory.

## Style Mandate

Use elevated but readable Bengali prose. Keep the voice stage-aware, riverine, political, and reflective. Treat uncertain claims about Lakshman Sen's Kanauj campaign as literary-historical possibilities rather than settled fact until research and correctness validate them.

## Chapter Structure

Every chapter ultimately resolves into three sections: Workshop, Story, and Discussion.

## Central Conflict

A community in modern Delhi tries to recover Bengal's historical self-respect through performance, while the inner novel tests whether military glory can survive scrutiny from common people, poets, priests, and the river itself.
