# Workshop Metadata

## Frame

The recurring present-day frame is the Delhi field and rehearsal culture of Pragati Opera after a successful Ramayana jatra production. Each session invites artists, actors, musicians, and organizers to debate which Bengali hero deserves the next major palā.

## Recurring Roles

- Mohit Kanjilal: director and organizer, pushing for a new Bengali historical production.
- Bishwarup Biswas: actor-playwright and the reader of the historical chapters.
- Ensemble artists: the questioning chorus that tests memory, nationalism, ethics, and stagecraft.

## Narrated Historical Figures

- Ballal Sen
- Lakshman Sen
- Mahasenapati Rudradev
- Malini
- Jayadeva
- Halayudha Mishra
- The envoy of Kanauj

## Sequence Notes

Each scaffolded chapter should be ready to host a Workshop / Story / Discussion flow. The workshop opens the question, the story carries the historical imagination, and the discussion measures moral and political consequence.

## Grounding Notes

- Era focus: late twelfth-century Bengal and the Gangetic north.
- Historical caution: treat the Kanauj campaign as literary-historical material until verified.
- River logic matters: logistics, trade, memory, and warfare should repeatedly pass through the Ganga.
