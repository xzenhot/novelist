# Theme Filter — Ridge

## Purpose
Assign the chapter's theme, theme essence, contemporary mapping, and stereotype selection. Ensure the thematic lens is woven into the chapter's images and turning points.

4. Urban Ecology as Ethics
5. Memory, Indigeneity, and Belonging

## Outputs
- Updated chapter `model.json` with `theme`, `theme_essence`, `contemporary_mapping`, `stereotype`
- `.space/pipeline/ridge/filters/theme/filter-summary.md`

## Rules
- One primary theme per chapter; a secondary theme may be noted.
- Contemporary mapping must connect the chapter's subject to a present-day ecological or urban concern.
- Stereotype selection: literary non-fiction, contemporary English register.
