## Oration

A seasonal stream that runs off the ridge becomes a figure for renewal and its fragility.

- The poem follows a monsoon stream that runs off the ridge, so that the seasonal water becomes a figure for renewal, and the reader is shown the brief, bright life of the stream.
- The stream is treated as a meditation on impermanence, so that the poet reflects on how the water comes and goes, and how its coming is a gift that the city too often buries.
- The monsoon stream is linked to the memory of the rivers the ridge once fed, so that the seasonal water is shown to be a remnant of a larger, older flow.
- The poem ends with the stream drying, so that the reader learns that renewal is fragile, and that to honor the stream is to honor what is brief and easily lost.
