## Oration

The hand-axe, the altar, the stepwell, the fort, the quarry — one continuous human record on one ridge.

- The poem assembles the hand-axe, the altar, the stepwell, the fort, and the quarry, so that they are seen as one continuous human record on one ridge, a single archive written in stone.
- The poet reflects on how each layer was made by different hands across deep time, so that the ridge becomes a text whose pages are strata, and the reader is taught to read it.
- The continuity is treated as the poem's central claim, so that the mountain is shown to be not a backdrop but the very spine of Delhi's human story.
- The poem ends with the record assembled, so that the reader learns that the mountain is an archive, and that to lose a layer is to lose a page of who Delhi has been.
