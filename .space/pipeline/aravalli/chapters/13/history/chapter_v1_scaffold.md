## Oration

The geological folding of the Aravalli becomes a figure for how time bends and layers itself.

- The poem reads the fold of the Aravalli — the buckling of ancient rock — as a figure for how time itself bends, so that a billion years are compressed into a single ridge the poet can walk.
- The fold is treated as a meditation on pressure: how the slow, immense force of continents produced the mountain, and how that same patience is absent from the city's haste.
- The poet links the geological fold to the folding of human memory, so that the strata of the ridge and the layers of history are shown to be one continuous bending.
- The poem ends with the poet tracing the fold with a hand, so that the reader learns that time is not a line but a fold, and that the mountain is where the fold becomes visible.
