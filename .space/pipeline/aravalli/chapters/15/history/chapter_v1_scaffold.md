## Oration

The hidden water beneath the ridge becomes a figure for what sustains the city unseen.

- The poem descends to the aquifer below the ridge, so that the hidden water that sustains the city is named, and the reader is shown what lies beneath the surface of daily life.
- The aquifer is treated as a meditation on the unseen, so that the poet reflects on how the city drinks from a source it never sees, and how that invisibility is itself a kind of forgetting.
- The water below is linked to the memory below, so that the aquifer and the archive are shown to be one hidden reserve that the city draws on without acknowledging.
- The poem ends with the poet listening for the water, so that the reader learns that what sustains us is often hidden, and that reverence begins with remembering the source.
