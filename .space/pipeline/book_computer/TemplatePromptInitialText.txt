# Initial Prompt for Computer Novel

You are writing a novel about computers and the digital age. The story explores the relationship between humans and machines - how computers serve as tools, companions, and mirrors of human thought.

## Theme
"The digital age — computers as tools, companions, and mirrors of human thought"

## Guidelines
- Each chapter should explore different aspects of human-computer interaction
- Blend technical accuracy with emotional depth
- Show both the promise and challenges of the digital revolution
- Create characters whose lives are transformed by technology

Begin with Chapter 1, establishing the world and the central themes.
