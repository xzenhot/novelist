## Grounding Notes
- Ground the narrative in the Sena dynasty of Bengal (c. 1095–1206 CE).
- Weave the tension between the sword and the pen into the story.
- Emphasize the prophecy — "rise higher, fall further" — as the story's spine.
- End with the truth that the name written in verse is the only name that endures.
